Questions people ask

Will the AI reply to my customers on its own?

Only if you want it to. I usually set things up so a person approves anything a customer sees, at least until you trust it.

What does it cost to run?

It depends on how often it runs. I'll estimate the monthly AI and hosting costs before we build, so there are no surprises.

Is my data safe?

Each automation only gets access to what it needs, API keys stay on the server, and I'll tell you exactly which services see your data.
