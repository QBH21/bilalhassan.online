Questions people ask

What's the difference between a website and a web app?

A website mostly shows information. A web app does work: people log in, enter data and get something back, like an invoice, a booking or a report.

Can it replace the spreadsheets we use now?

Often, yes. I'd look at your sheets first and tell you honestly what should move into the app and what's fine where it is.

Where does it run?

Small tools run well on low-cost hosting. The accounts are set up in your name, so the app and its data stay yours.
