Questions people ask

How long does a website take?

It depends on how many pages you need and how ready your words and photos are. We agree on a timeline before I start, and you see previews the whole way through, so nothing is a surprise at the end.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the pages and features. You get a price before any work starts, and it only changes if we both agree to change what's being built.

Can you redesign the site I already have?

Yes. I start by testing your current site with Google's Lighthouse, so we both know what's slow or broken before anything changes, and we keep whatever already works.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell me about your business and your customers, and I handle the rest, in plain English or Urdu.

Let's build your website

Tell me what you sell, who buys it and when you'd like to launch. I'll reply with honest next steps.
