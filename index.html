How I built Kavoor

Kavoor is a perfume store I designed and built. Keep scrolling and you'll watch it go from an empty file to the live site.

  1. 1StructurePlain HTML first: every heading, link and button in the right order, before any design.
  2. 2LayoutThe grid, spacing and hierarchy, worked out at phone size first.
  3. 3StyleType, colour and the small details that make it look like Kavoor.
  4. 4MotionSmall movements that show what you can tap and what just happened.
  5. 5SpeedI trimmed the images, fonts and code until it scored 99 on Google's Lighthouse test for phones.
  6. 6LiveThat's the real kavoor.store, live now.
